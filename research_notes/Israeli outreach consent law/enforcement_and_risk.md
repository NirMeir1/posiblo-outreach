# Enforcement and litigation risk under Communications Law s.30A (Israel) for a one-time consent-request outreach

Scope note. Research done 27 Nov 2026. Primary texts were read in full where marked "read in full" (court PDFs downloaded and extracted with pdftotext). Everything else is marked as secondary or UNKNOWN. On the coordinator's instruction, no attempt was made to get around any HTTP 403. The Ministry of Communications spam FAQ on gov.il (https://www.gov.il/he/departments/faq/17052018_7) was NOT read; it is listed as "not readable (403)" in the brief and was not retried. Two gov.il BlobFolder PDFs (Ministry of Justice freedom-of-information answers) loaded normally with a plain request and were read.

Binding vs non-binding: only Supreme Court rulings bind lower courts (Basic Law: The Judiciary s.20(b)). Rulings of a single Supreme Court justice in a third-instance leave to appeal (e.g. Glasberg) carry less weight than a three-justice panel (e.g. Hazani). Rulings of district, magistrate and small-claims courts bind no one.

Statute: s.30A text used is the current text supplied in the brief (scratchpad file s30a.txt, last amended by amendment 76, 2022).

## E1. Damages courts actually award per message under s.30A(י)

### Takeaway
The binding rule (Supreme Court, three justices, Hazani 2014) is that ₪1,000 per message is a ceiling, and the starting point for a claim over a single violation is the full ₪1,000. Courts may award less per message as the count rises, and they must also award costs high enough to make a small claim worthwhile. In lower-court rulings read for this research, awards ranged from ₪0 (claim dismissed) to ₪1,200 total for one message. Per-message awards on multi-message claims ran from ₪33 to ₪600. The same Supreme Court ruling also held that a message which itself contains advertising is NOT a permitted one-time consent request.

### Cited Findings

**Supreme Court (binding)**

- **רע"א 1954/14 אילן חזני נ' שמעון הנגבי (סיתונית מועדון דאיה ורחיפה במצנחים), Supreme Court.** Panel: Justices A. Rubinstein, U. Fogelman and Y. Amit. Decided "ח' באב התשע"ד (4.8.2014)". Lead opinion by Fogelman. The case number and holding were verified from the full text (read in full). — [Supreme Court ruling PDF, hosted by Univ. of Haifa](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf); also hosted at [law.co.il](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/ilan_hazani_5Eq1fpp.pdf)
  - The court heard it "as if leave had been granted" because of its general importance. Being a three-justice panel (the case began in the magistrate's court), it is the leading binding precedent on s.30A(י). — [Hazani, para 1](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Purpose of the damages: "מטרת הפיצוי אינה להשיב את מצבו של התובע לקדמותו ... כי אם לאכוף את החוק, להרתיע את הנתבע ולתמרץ הגשת תביעות יעילות. זהו אפוא פיצוי לדוגמה". — [Hazani, para 9](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Starting point and the gradual scale: the number of messages sets the ceiling, but it is only one factor. "אפשר שייפסק פיצוי בסכום זהה לאדם שנשלחו לו 100 הודעות ולאדם שנשלחו לו 1,000 הודעות ... נקודת המוצא היא שתביעה בגין הפרה בודדת תזכה את התובע במלוא הסכום (1,000 ש"ח) כדי שתושג התכלית ההרתעתית". — [Hazani, para 10](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Costs: courts should award costs and legal fees that make the claim worthwhile, even above the damages. "אף שתביעה בגין הפרה בודדת תזכה את התובע לכל היותר בפיצוי של 1,000 ש"ח, ניתן לפסוק בגינה שכר טרחה והוצאות העולים על סכום זה כדי שיהיה כדאי להגישה". The court added that for a single violation heard at one hearing in small claims, "אפשר שדי בסכום הפיצוי". — [Hazani, para 10](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Facts: 27 e-mails sent to the claimant's 13 addresses (two per address, three to one). Each e-mail carried the footer "המייל נשלח על פי החוק החדש לרשימה עסקית באופן חד פעמי ...". The trial court awarded ₪1,000 in total and the district court upheld that. — [Hazani, paras 11–13](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Result: ₪10,000 in damages (of a ₪27,000 ceiling), plus costs and ₪15,000 legal fees. Factors: the advertising was "שאינו פוגעני"; the defendant's good-faith belief that this was lawful was not disproved; on the other side, the violation was repeated, and the defendant ran a broad defence (claiming the plaintiff sent the e-mails to himself), which made the case longer and more costly. — [Hazani, para 18](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - Removal option: whether the sender offered an easy way to unsubscribe does not affect liability. Fogelman held that by itself it should not reduce the damages. Rubinstein (citing his own Glasberg ruling, para י"ג) said a court may weigh it, and that the difference between the two views probably has little practical effect. — [Hazani, para 17 and concurring opinions](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
  - **Directly relevant to the outreach plan (para 16):** the court quoted the one-time business exception and said: "ברם פנייה הכוללת כשלעצמה דבר פרסומת אינה יכולה להיחשב פנייה מהסוג המותר בסעיף. אם לא נאמר כך, נימצא מאפשרים לכל מפרסם לשלוח הודעת פרסומת אחת שמשולבת בה גם הצעה לקבל דברי פרסומת בעתיד ... ההצעה שסעיף 30א(ב) סיפה מדבר בה היא הצעה 'ניטראלית' שלא כרוכה בה פרסומת של המוצר או השירות שהשולח מבקש לפרסם". The court also noted that the question of what counts as a "בית עסק" was not argued below, and it assumed for the purpose of the ruling that the claimant's addresses were a business. — [Hazani, para 16](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
- **רע"א 2904/14 גלסברג נ' קלאב רמון בע"מ, Supreme Court, Justice A. Rubinstein sitting alone, 27.7.2014.** Hazani describes it as a comprehensive ruling on the same question, decided by a single justice because it came from a small-claims judgment. I did not read its full text. Its content here comes from Hazani and from lower courts that cite it: the easy-removal factor may be weighed (para י"ג), and the statutory sum is a basis that "can be reduced in suitable circumstances". — [Hazani, para 1 and concurrence](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf); [Zorea small-claims ruling citing Glasberg](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/zorea.pdf)
- **רע"א 335/23 דאבלטק בע"מ נ' לב, Supreme Court, Justice E. Baron, 27.7.2023.** A class action, not individual damages; see E2. — [ruling PDF on law.co.il](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/dabltech.pdf)

### Inferences
- For one message to one business, the realistic exposure in an individual small claim is about ₪0–1,200 in damages plus costs (in the rulings read, costs were ₪350–1,500). Hazani's "starting point = full ₪1,000 for a single violation" means a court can award the full amount.
- The decisive question is not the amount but liability: whether the message is a "neutral" offer to agree (exempt) or contains advertising (a violation from the first message). Hazani (binding) and Maoz show that a message describing or promoting the service can fall outside the exception. Whether a consent request that names the field ("שירותי אוטומציה ו-AI לעסקים") but makes no offer, price or call to buy is "neutral" has not been decided in any ruling I found. That is a lawyer question (G9).
- I found no ruling in which the defendant sent a pure, neutral consent request and was held liable.

### Gaps
- Glasberg (רע"א 2904/14) full text not read. Its holdings are reported here only through Hazani and lower courts.
- No later Supreme Court ruling on individual s.30A damages (2015–2026) was found in the searches. UNKNOWN whether one exists.
- Hadera Small Claims 35387-09-11 Yigal Ram v. Geva Barat (2011) and Bat Yam 49111-10-22 Keinan v. Eshel Jerusalem (2023), cited in the brief, could not be found online in these searches. Not verified.
- No systematic statistics on award amounts exist in an official source that I found. The isoc.org.il spam ruling database did not come up in any search result.

## E2. Class actions (תובענות ייצוגיות) under the spam law

### Takeaway
Class actions under s.30A are expressly allowed: item 12 of the Second Schedule to the Class Actions Law 2006, as stated by the Supreme Court in Hazani and by the Haifa District Court in Cohen v. Cellcom. They are certified in practice, and they are brought even over one SMS sent once to many recipients. In Ben David v. Isracard, a single erroneous SMS to about 54,000 people led to a class action and an approved settlement. Settlements and fees are in the hundreds of thousands of shekels. With a few hundred recipients receiving one neutral consent request each, a class action is a theoretical but low-probability risk. Its real driver is whether the message is held to be advertising.

### Cited Findings
- Hazani (Supreme Court): "חוק תובענות ייצוגיות, התשס"ו-2006 קובע כי ניתן לנהל תובענה ייצוגית בעילה זו (פרט 12 לתוספת השנייה לחוק זה)". The court also observed that class remedies generally require proof of damage (Class Actions Law s.20(ה)). It left open whether exemplary damages can be awarded in a class action ("שאלה היא אם..."). — [Hazani, paras 7, 10](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
- **ת"צ 23787-03-23 כהן נ' סלקום ישראל בע"מ, Haifa District Court, Judge M. Ali, 19.12.2024.** The motion was filed under "פריט 12 לתוספת השנייה לחוק תובענות ייצוגיות ... שמאפשר הגשת תובענה ייצוגית נגד 'מפרסם'". **Certified**: "התקיימו התנאים לאישורה של התובענה כייצוגית ומחליט לקבל את הבקשה". The class is customers who gave details during a purchase and were sent ads over 7 years. The court held Cellcom failed the s.30A(ג) opt-out condition, and it said a recipient's duty to act may affect the amount of damage but does not rule out a class. — [Cohen v. Cellcom decision](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/cohen90.pdf)
- **ת"צ 53692-12-21 בן דוד נ' ישראכרט מימון בע"מ, Jerusalem Magistrate's Court, Judge M. Benki, 3.9.2024.** **One** promotional SMS sent on 3.10.2021 because of a "תקלה נקודתית". The class was 54,458 recipients without consent. The settlement was approved: 6,640 vouchers worth about ₪50 each (about ₪332,000), donated to a terror-victims' organisation, plus a ₪13,280 reward to the claimant and ₪53,120 + VAT in fees. The court noted the need to "להיזהר מניצול לרעה של מנגנון התובענה הייצוגית ולהבחין בין מי שאכן הפר את החוק ביודעין או אף ברשלנות ובין מי שהפעיל מנגנוני מניעה". — [Ben David v. Isracard](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/ben_david25.pdf)
- **רע"א 335/23 דאבלטק בע"מ נ' לב, Supreme Court (Justice E. Baron), 27.7.2023.** A spam class action (the Haifa District Court, ת"צ 24822-02-20) over e-mails sent to a dating site's old registrants. The district court rejected two settlements because of a gap of about ₪4.5M between the offered relief and the claimed relief (₪100 × 52,154 messages = ₪5,215,400). The Supreme Court allowed the appeal and approved the amended settlement, with value assessed at ₪1,233,420. It set class counsel's fee at ₪150,000 + VAT. — [Dabltech ruling](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/dabltech.pdf)

### Inferences
- A class needs a common cause and a class large enough to justify the costs. Under the plan (a few hundred businesses, one message each, an exemption claimed), the most a class could plausibly claim is in the low hundreds of thousands of shekels, and only if the message is held to be advertising. The cases found all involve tens of thousands of recipients and large companies. I found no class action against a small sender with a few hundred recipients. That is an absence of evidence, not proof that it cannot happen.
- Keeping the volume small, and the text clearly neutral and identical for every recipient, reduces both the probability and the size of a class claim. At the same time, an identical text makes the common question easy to show if it is held unlawful.

### Gaps
- No Supreme Court ruling was found that decides whether exemplary damages under s.30A(י) are available to a class. Hazani left the question open. UNKNOWN.
- No class action was found that concerns business recipients or a one-time consent request.
- The Class Actions Law schedule text itself (item 12 wording) was not read from Nevo or the Knesset. It is reported here via Hazani and Cohen v. Cellcom.

## E3. Criminal fines under s.30A(ו) and enforcement in practice

### Takeaway
s.30A(ו)(1) (sending without consent) → Penal Law s.61(א)(4): up to ₪226,000. s.30A(ו)(2) (missing mandatory details) and s.30A(ח) (officer's duty to supervise) → s.61(א)(3): up to ₪75,300. These amounts appear in the Penal Law text on Wikisource; I did not confirm on Nevo that they are current as of 2026. In practice, criminal enforcement of s.30A appears to be nil: as of July 2020, the Ministry of Justice found **no** indictment under s.30A in its computerised system. I found no information on administrative enforcement by the Ministry of Communications.

### Cited Findings
- s.30A(ו)(1): "מפרסם המשגר דבר פרסומת בניגוד להוראות סעיפים קטנים (ב) או (ג), דינו – קנס כאמור בסעיף 61(א)(4) לחוק העונשין". s.30A(ו)(2): missing or misleading s.30A(ה) details → "קנס כאמור בסעיף 61(א)(3)". s.30A(ח): an officer of a corporation who fails to supervise → s.61(א)(3), with a presumption of breach "אלא אם כן עשה כל שניתן". s.30A(ז): when setting a fine, a court may take into account exemplary damages already awarded. — [s.30A current text, brief source file] (scratchpad law-dl/s30a.txt, from Nevo, per the brief)
- Penal Law s.61(א): "(3) אם קבוע לעבירה עונש מאסר למעלה משנה ולא יותר משלוש שנים – קנס עד 75,300 שקלים חדשים; (4) אם קבוע לעבירה עונש מאסר למעלה משלוש שנים – קנס עד 226,000 שקלים חדשים". Also (1) ₪14,400 and (2) ₪29,200. — [Penal Law on Hebrew Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9F) (a community transcription, not an official source)
- A labour-court sentencing ruling also states the s.61(א)(4) maximum as ₪226,000 per offence. — [workrights.co.il](https://www.workrights.co.il/2543-09) (secondary)
- Hamichlol says its Penal Law amounts are "correct for 2013", and Penal Law s.64 allows the amounts to be updated by order. So the figures could in principle have changed. I found no source showing an update after 2013. — [Hamichlol "קנס"](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%A1) (secondary)
- **Criminal enforcement in practice:** Ministry of Justice freedom-of-information answer, file 2015-20-0240, 1.7.2020. Indictments in which one offence was s.30 OR s.30A of the Communications Law: 111 from 2014 to 1.5.2020. But: "במערכת הממוחשבת לא נמצאו כתבי אישום לפי סעיף 30א לחוק התקשורת". So all of them were under s.30 (harassment via telecom equipment), not under the spam section. — [MoJ FOI answer 2015-20-0240](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/answer-freedom-information-13/he/%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D%202015-20-0240.pdf)
- A second MoJ FOI answer (2015-21-0221, 2.6.2021) counts 73 indictments under s.30 from 1.1.2019 to 19.4.2021, about harassment, including over WhatsApp. It does not concern s.30A. — [MoJ FOI answer 2015-21-0221](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/justice2015-21-0221/he/%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91%D7%94%20%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%94%202015-21-0221%20%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D.pdf)
- Ministry of Communications spam FAQ: not readable (403). Not retried, per instructions. — https://www.gov.il/he/departments/faq/17052018_7

### Inferences
- s.30A sets no administrative fine (no עיצום כספי) in the text read. Enforcement is criminal (in theory) and civil (in practice). This matches the Supreme Court's description in Hazani of private claims as the main enforcement tool.
- For a small sender of a few hundred neutral consent requests, criminal exposure is theoretical. The practical risk is civil.

### Gaps
- Official current s.61 amounts (Nevo or Knesset consolidated text, 2026): not confirmed. Treat ₪75,300 and ₪226,000 as "most recent figures found".
- s.30A indictments after May 2020: UNKNOWN.
- Ministry of Communications enforcement activity (warnings, complaints handling): UNKNOWN. The FAQ was blocked and no other official source was found.

## E4. Responding to a demand letter (מכתב התראה) or a small-claims suit

### Takeaway
No statute or official guideline prescribes a response. The case law, however, consistently rewards quick removal, apology, proof of good faith and a cooperative defence, and it punishes broad, combative defences. In Hazani, the Supreme Court raised the award partly because the defendant ran a broad defence that prolonged the case.

### Cited Findings
- Avraham v. Hasson (Rehovot Small Claims, 28.3.2024): 15 SMS → ₪500 total, no costs. The factors were "הסרת התובע מרשימת הדיוור עם קבלת הפניה, התנצלות הנתבע והפקת הלקחים המידית, השגת מטרות החוק עוד טרם הגשת התביעה". — [Avraham ruling](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/avraham5.pdf)
- Hazani (Supreme Court): an aggravating factor was that "המשיב פרס יריעת הגנה רחבה" (claiming the plaintiff sent the mails himself), which required a court expert and raised costs. Good faith that was not disproved worked in the defendant's favour. — [Hazani, para 18](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
- Maoz (Petah Tikva Small Claims, 2015): the award was reduced from the maximum because the sender acted in good faith on a mailing company's explanation. The defendant's failure to appear at the first hearing produced a default judgment and extra costs. — [Maoz](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/adi_maoz2_4RgtsCC.pdf)
- Zorea (Tel Aviv Small Claims, 2026): when a recipient denies consent, "עובר הנטל אל כתפי המפרסם להוכיח כי ניתנה הסכמה מפורשת". A third party's letter was not enough without documentary proof (a sign-up form) or a witness. — [Zorea](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/zorea.pdf)
- Meiri (Rishon LeZion Magistrate, 2017): the defendant won by showing documents (an advertising agency's letter and the manager's affidavit) that it had no link to the mailer. — [Meiri](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/meiri9.pdf)
- Zaides (Tel Aviv Small Claims, 2023): the parties were sent to court mediation on the hearing day. It failed, but this shows mediation is offered. — [Zaides](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/zaides.pdf)

### Inferences (practical, not legal advice)
- On receiving a demand letter: (1) do not ignore it and do not miss deadlines or hearings (Maoz); (2) immediately add the sender's domain, e-mail and phone to the suppression list and confirm the removal in writing (Avraham); (3) pull the exact record for that site: the timestamp, the exact template version text, the form URL, and proof that only one message was sent; (4) reply briefly and factually: one message only, a neutral consent request under s.30A(ב) last sentence, the recipient is a business, the removal was done, nothing further will be sent; (5) do not make aggressive or factually doubtful defences (Hazani); (6) consider a small goodwill settlement where the cost of defending is higher. Paying is not an admission if it is stated as "without prejudice", but the effect of that wording should be confirmed with a lawyer.
- If a lawyer is retained for small claims, note that representation by a lawyer in small claims normally requires the court's permission (Small Claims Procedure). This was not verified in this research, so ask the lawyer.

### Gaps
- No ruling found where a demand letter answered with an offer of removal or settlement was later discussed as reducing damages specifically. The evidence is post-suit conduct.
- The small-claims ceiling for 2026: one secondary site says ₪39,900. — [Bizportal calculator](https://www.bizportal.co.il/additives/429/spam-claim.html) (secondary; not verified against the official court-administration figure).

## E5. Defences: lawful one-time consent requests, serial plaintiffs, rebutting "ביודעין"

### Takeaway
(a) I found **no** ruling that held a one-time consent request to a business lawful on its facts, and none that defined what makes such a request lawful beyond the Supreme Court's negative test: the request must be "neutral" and contain no advertising of the product or service (Hazani). (b) Being a "serial plaintiff" is not by itself a ground to reduce damages, but it can combine with bad faith, delay, or failing to sue the real sender. (c) The "ביודעין" presumption can be rebutted, except in the three statutory cases in s.30A(י)(5); one magistrate's court ruling shows how.

### Cited Findings
- One-time business exception, binding interpretation: "ההצעה שסעיף 30א(ב) סיפה מדבר בה היא הצעה 'ניטראלית' שלא כרוכה בה פרסומת של המוצר או השירות שהשולח מבקש לפרסם". — [Hazani, para 16](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
- Maoz applied this to a single e-mail to a lawyer, which offered toner: the exception does not apply because the message contained the advertising itself, "מבלי לשלוח באותו שלב גם את תוכן הפרסום עצמו, וזאת עד שתתקבל הסכמת בית העסק". — [Maoz](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/adi_maoz2_4RgtsCC.pdf)
- Serial plaintiffs: Meiri (Rishon LeZion Magistrate, 2017) quotes another court: "גם בית המשפט העליון בעניין חזני נתן דעתו לקיומם של תובעים סדרתיים ... ובהחלט לא ניתן לומר כי הוא ראה ב'סדרתיות' כאמור שיקול מכריע להפחתת פיצוי". In Meiri itself, the claimant's serial filing, a five-year delay and choosing not to sue the actual mailer counted against him. — [Meiri](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/meiri9.pdf)
- In Hazani, the claimant (an economist and lawyer with 13 addresses, 6–7 earlier similar claims) still won ₪10,000 + ₪15,000 fees in the Supreme Court. — [Hazani, paras 11–12, 18](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf)
- Freifeld (Akko, 2024): the defendants called the claimant a "תובע סדרתי" who failed to click unsubscribe. The court still awarded ₪350/msg. — [Freifeld](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/freifeld17.pdf)
- Rebutting "ביודעין": s.30A(י)(5) presumes knowledge "אלא אם כן הוכיח אחרת". There is no defence if the message was sent after a refusal, if the advertiser previously sent an unlawful message to the same recipient, or if addresses were generated randomly. — s.30A(י)(5) text (brief source file). In Meiri, the court held that a letter from the defendant's advertising agency plus the manager's affidavit that there was no link to the mailer "יש בהם כדי להרים את נטל ההוכחה ... די בו כדי לסתור החזקה". The claim was dismissed. — [Meiri, paras 15–20](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/meiri9.pdf)
- Recipient's own conduct: in Ben Yosef, a claimant business that started the relationship and kept 114 messages for 6 years was awarded nothing. — [Ben Yosef](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/ben_yosef2.pdf)

### Inferences
- For Posiblo, the "ביודעין" rebuttal is unlikely to help: the owner sends knowingly. The live defence is "no violation at all", because the message is a neutral one-time offer to a business. That depends on (i) the recipient being a "בית עסק" (not decided by the Supreme Court; Hazani assumed it), (ii) the message being "neutral", and (iii) "one-time" being true. The ledger provides evidence for (iii).
- The second statutory bar, "sent in the past an unlawful message to the same recipient", means any second message to a business that never agreed removes the knowledge defence. This is consistent with the send-once rule.

### Gaps
- The brief's Hadera ruling (35387-09-11, first approach lawful, second unlawful) was not found online and is not verified.
- No ruling found on whether naming the service field ("automation and AI services") in a consent request makes it advertising.

## Personal liability under s.30A(ח) and where claims are filed

### Takeaway
s.30A(ח) is a criminal supervision duty of a corporation's officers, with a presumption of breach. In civil practice, a small-claims court refused to hold a sole director and shareholder personally liable without a separate cause of action. For a sole proprietor (עוסק), the owner is the advertiser, so personal liability is direct whatever s.30A(ח) says. Individual s.30A claims are overwhelmingly heard in small-claims courts; this is based on the sample, not on statistics.

### Cited Findings
- s.30A(ח): "מנהל של תאגיד וכן מי שאחראי לתחומי השיווק או הפרסום בתאגיד ... חייב לפקח ולעשות כל שניתן למניעת עבירה ... המפר הוראה זו, דינו – קנס כאמור בסעיף 61(א)(3)". — s.30A text (brief source file)
- Freifeld (Akko, 7.11.2024): the claim against the sole shareholder and director was dismissed: "לא בוססה כל עילת תביעה כנגד הנתבע 2 באופן אישי ... אף אם הוא מכהן כדירקטור ובעל מניות יחיד בחברה". The claimant had to pay him ₪1,500 costs. The court cited ת"ק (קריות) 23742-11-14 חונדיא נ' אור הקסם בע"מ (15.6.2015) on proving officers' personal liability. — [Freifeld](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/freifeld17.pdf)
- Forum: of the 10 individual-claim rulings read, 7 were small-claims courts and 2 were magistrate's courts (Hazani began in the Herzliya magistrate's court). Class actions were in district or magistrate's courts. — sources in the E1 table and E2.

### Inferences
- An incorporated company would mostly shield the owner from civil s.30A damages (Freifeld). The criminal s.30A(ח) presumption against officers remains, though its practical weight is low given zero s.30A indictments found. Whether to incorporate for this reason is a lawyer or accountant decision.

### Gaps
- No official statistics on how many s.30A claims are filed per year or by which court. UNKNOWN.

## G8 input: risk-reduction recommendations

### Takeaway
The main legal risk is that a court classifies the message as advertising rather than a "neutral" consent offer (Hazani). Every other measure only reduces the amount. A small pilot, conservative neutral wording, strict once-only sending with proof, and fast handling of removals and complaints keep the expected cost to occasional small claims of about ₪0–1,200 plus costs each.

### Cited Findings
- Neutrality is the liability test (Hazani para 16); a single message that includes advertising is a violation (Maoz). — [Hazani](https://law.haifa.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14019540.M05.pdf); [Maoz](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/adi_maoz2_4RgtsCC.pdf)
- Courts weigh good faith, prompt removal, apology and the scope of the violation (s.30A(י)(3)(ג) "היקף ההפרה"; Avraham; Zorea). — [Avraham](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/avraham5.pdf); [Zorea](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/zorea.pdf)
- The burden of proving consent, and the facts of the message, falls on the advertiser (Zorea). — [Zorea](https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/zorea.pdf)
- A second unlawful message removes the knowledge defence (s.30A(י)(5)(ב)), and a message after a refusal has no defence (s.30A(י)(5)(א)). — s.30A text
- Class-action exposure scales with the number of recipients (Ben David: 54,458 recipients; Dabltech: 52,154 messages). — [Ben David](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/ben_david25.pdf); [Dabltech](https://law.co.il/media/computer-law/dabltech.pdf)

### Inferences (recommendations, not legal advice)
1. **Wording first.** Strip anything that could read as advertising content: no benefits, no offer, no price, no link, no description beyond the minimum topic. Ask the lawyer the yes/no question: "Does naming the topic 'מידע על שירותי אוטומציה ו-AI לעסקים' make the message a דבר פרסומת rather than a neutral offer under Hazani para 16?"
2. **Pilot batch.** Send to 20–30 businesses at most, then pause 2–4 weeks to watch for complaints, removals or demand letters before scaling. This number is my judgment, not from a source.
3. **Volume caps.** Keep the total in the low hundreds and spread it over time (for example, at most about 25 per day). That keeps any hypothetical class small and limits the "היקף ההפרה" factor. Also my judgment.
4. **Records to keep per site, with no expiry while claims are possible.** The exact rendered message text and template version; UTC timestamp; channel and target URL or address; proof the recipient is a business (a snapshot of the site showing the business identity, such as a registration number, if any); HTTP response or confirmation; the ledger row showing one send only; the suppression-list entry and its date for any "הסר". Civil limitation is generally 7 years (Prescription Law); Cohen v. Cellcom defined its class over 7 years. This supports keeping minimal proof records for 7 years. The general 7-year period itself was not verified in this research.
5. **Demand-letter procedure.** Suppress the address at once. Reply within days with the facts: a single neutral consent request, the business recipient, the removal done, the record attached. Never send again. Calendar every deadline and hearing. Take no combative factual positions. Consider a modest goodwill settlement against defence costs.
6. **Never a second message** to any business that did not answer "כן". Under s.30A(י)(5)(ב), a second message removes the knowledge defence; under Hazani, each message is a separate violation.
7. **Insurance.** UNKNOWN. I found no source on whether Israeli professional or third-party liability policies cover statutory s.30A damages. Ask an insurance broker; exemplary damages are often excluded, but that is unverified.
8. **Entity choice.** Freifeld suggests a company shields the owner from civil damages, but s.30A(ח) puts criminal duties on officers. This is a lawyer question.

### Gaps
- The pilot size, daily cap and retention period are judgments, not sourced rules.
- Insurance coverage: no source found.
- The Ministry of Communications position (FAQ): not readable (403).
