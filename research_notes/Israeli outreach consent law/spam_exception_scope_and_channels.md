# Scope of the one-time business exception in section 30A(ב) of the Communications (Bezeq and Broadcasting) Law 1982, and which channels it covers

Research date: 27 November 2026. Israel only.

Source notes that apply throughout:
- Statute text: current section 30A as extracted from Nevo (law page `https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm`, saved locally by the coordinator and read in full). Nevo's heading for section 30A lists only amendments 40 (2008) and 76 (2022) as the amendments in the current text (plus 63, 66 and 72 on individual sub-sections).
- Not readable: the Ministry of Communications spam FAQ `https://www.gov.il/he/pages/17052018_7` (and `/he/departments/faq/17052018_7`) returned HTTP 403 to automated access. It was not bypassed. A search-engine snippet only says the FAQ calls the business one-time approach "the first exception" to the consent rule. That snippet was not verified against the page.
- Not found: the printed explanatory notes (דברי הסבר) of the bill that became amendment 40. Nevo cites the bill as "ה"ח 182". The Knesset committee transcripts for that bill were found and read instead (below). They are primary Knesset sources, but they record debate, not the official notes.
- Binding precedent: no Supreme Court ruling that interprets the one-time business exception was found. One Supreme Court decision quotes the exception without interpreting it (רע"א 3599/18, below). All other rulings found are non-binding (small claims, or a district-court decision refusing leave to appeal).

## Overall: is there binding interpretation, legislative history, or a 2025–2026 change affecting the exception?

### Takeaway
No Supreme Court or district-court ruling interpreting the business exception was found. The best interpretive material is the 2007 Knesset committee transcripts. They show the exception was meant as the "European opt-in" balance: you may make one first approach to a business, asking for consent, with no advertising in it. Amendment 83 (July 2026) does not amend section 30A.

### Cited Findings
- Current statute, last sentence of 30A(ב): "פנייה חד-פעמית מטעם מפרסם לנמען שהוא בית עסק או לנמען לשם קבלת תרומה או תעמולה, באחת הדרכים האמורות בסעיף קטן זה, המהווה הצעה להסכים לקבל דברי פרסומת מטעמו, לא תיחשב הפרה של הוראות סעיף זה." The "דרכים" listed in the same sub-section are "פקסימיליה, מערכת חיוג אוטומטי, הודעה אלקטרונית או הודעת מסר קצר". — [Nevo, Communications Law, s. 30A](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Origin: before amendment 40, section 30A covered fax only and already had the business exception. Nevo's version history quotes the old text: "פניה חד-פעמית מטעם משגר לנמען שהינו בית עסק, הכוללת הצעה להסכים לקבלת דברי פרסומת מטעמו, באמצעות פקסימילה, לא תהיה עבירה לפי סעיף זה." Amendment 40 (ס"ח תשס"ח מס' 2153, 1.6.2008, p. 518; in force 1.12.2008) replaced section 30A and extended the rule to automatic dialing, electronic messages and SMS. — [Nevo, s. 30A version history](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm). The published amendment is also on the Knesset site (found, not read): [fs.knesset.gov.il 17_lsr_299991.pdf](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_299991.pdf)
- Amendment 63 (2016) added the words "או לנמען לשם קבלת תרומה או תעמולה". The 2016 text in Nevo's history shows this. — [Nevo, s. 30A version history](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- The Ministry of Justice explained the rationale in the joint Economics / Science committee on 31 January 2007 (the bill was then "הצעת חוק התקשורת (בזק ושידורים) (תיקון מס' 33), התשס"ה-2005"): "הצעת החוק שלנו מגלמת את ההסדר על פי המודל האירופי, שמכונה מודל ה-opt-in... על מנת לשלוח דבר פרסומת לאדם שהוא אדם פרטי ולא בית עסק, אתה מחויב לבקש את ההסכמה שלו מראש. לגבי בית עסק, האיזון קצת יותר מקל, באופן שמותר לשלוח לבית עסק הצעה חד-פעמית ולקבל את הסכמתו כדי לשלוח פרסומת. הכלל הזה חל על כל פרסומת שנשלחת באמצעות פקסימיליה, מערכת חיוג אוטומטית, הודעת דואר אלקטרוני או דף מסר קצר". — [Knesset committee transcript no. 2, 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)
- The same transcript, on the balance chosen: "הוא כן מאפשר פנייה חד-פעמית לקבל הסכמה של בית עסק. לעומת זאת, הפנייה לצרכן פרטי נתפסת כמשהו אחר... הטלמרקטינג האנושי נתפס כמשהו שפחות הפך למכת מדינה, ולכן הוא עדיין אפשרי." — [Knesset committee transcript, 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)
- In the same session a participant warned that senders were already abusing a "first message": "הם לא שולחים את אותה הודעה ראשונה רק כדי לקבל הסכמה, אלא הם שולחים הודעה שאומרת שהם מבקשים לקבל את ההסכמה להמשיך ולקבל מיילים בנוגע לחופשות מדהימות באנטליה וכולי, ובעצם כל הפרסומת באותה הודעה ראשונה." — [Knesset committee transcript, 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)
- In the joint committee on 27 November 2007, the Ministry of Communications described the first exception: "יש סייג שהאיסור לא יכלול הצעה ראשונית חד-פעמית לבית עסק שמבקש את הסכמתו לקבלת דבר הפרסומת באמצעים שהזכרנו". A member stressed: "מדובר בבית עסק, לא בית מגורים" and was answered "נכון, בית עסק בלבד". Another participant read the text as an offer "בלי הפרסומת עצמה. כתוב כאן: 'המהווה הצעה להסכים לקבלת דברי פרסומת מטעמו'". — [Knesset committee transcript no. 3, 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- Supreme Court: רע"א 3599/18 שפירא נ' עופרי (Justice D. Barak-Erez, 30.5.2018) quoted the business exception ("פנייה חד-פעמית מטעם מפרסם לנמען שהוא בית עסק") but did not interpret it. It dismissed the leave application. It described Israeli law as having adopted "הגישה המחמירה" (opt-in). — [text of the decision hosted by Afik & Co.](https://he.afiklaw.com/caselaw/15724). This is a law-firm site hosting the decision; it was not checked against supreme.court.gov.il.
- Amendment 83 (ס"ח תשפ"ו מס' 3572, 28.7.2026, p. 1026, ה"ח 1898): in Nevo's annotated text, all its changes take effect on 28.7.2028. They concern section 1 definitions and broadcasting matters: for example, adding "אספקת תכנים", "ספק תכנים" and "חוק השידורים", deleting "בעל רישיון לשידורים", and changing "ציוד קצה". No amendment-83 annotation appears anywhere in section 30A. — [Nevo, Communications Law](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Pending private bills found that would extend 30A to "flash" SMS (פ/4522/25, פ/5163/25, 2024). Their status was not checked and they were not read in full. — [law.co.il, flash-SMS bill](https://www.law.co.il/legislation/2024/04/06/spam-law-amendment-private-bill-flash-sms/)

### Inferences
- Amendment 83 changes definitions in section 1 such as "ציוד קצה". Section 30A's heading uses "מיתקן בזק או ציוד קצה", and the definition of "מערכת חיוג אוטומטי" uses "מיתקן בזק או ציוד קצה". None of the visible changes seems aimed at spam, and they apply only from 28.7.2028. So for an outreach run in 2026–2027, section 30A as quoted above is the operative text.
- The legislative history fits a strict reading: one approach, to a business, that only asks for consent and contains no advertising.

### Gaps
- The official 2005 or 2008 explanatory notes (ה"ח 182) were not located or read.
- Still to do: read the gov.il FAQ by hand in a browser (it blocked automated access).
- The full text of amendment 83 was not read, only Nevo's annotations. A manual check of ס"ח 3572 would confirm it leaves section 30A alone.
- Whether any 2025–2026 spam bill passed a reading: not checked beyond the flash-SMS bills.

## A1: Does one message per business, by contact form or email, fall under the 30A(ב) exception?

### Takeaway
Email to a recipient that is clearly a business: OK on the statute's own words, provided the message is a pure consent request (see the brief's B1) and truly the only approach. The contact form is GRAY until A2 is settled. No binding ruling confirms or narrows the exception, so the overall verdict is GRAY leaning OK.

### Cited Findings
- The exception has these elements in the statute: (1) "פנייה חד-פעמית"; (2) "מטעם מפרסם"; (3) "לנמען שהוא בית עסק"; (4) "באחת הדרכים האמורות בסעיף קטן זה", that is, fax, automatic dialing, "הודעה אלקטרונית" or "הודעת מסר קצר"; (5) "המהווה הצעה להסכים לקבל דברי פרסומת מטעמו". — [Nevo, s. 30A(ב)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Email is an "הודעה אלקטרונית": "מסר בזק מקודד המועבר ברשת האינטרנט אל נמען או קבוצה של נמענים, וניתן לשמירה ולאחזור בדרך ממוחשבת". The Ministry of Justice told the Knesset the rule covers "הודעת דואר אלקטרוני". — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm); [Knesset transcript 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)
- The courts' general approach is strict and literal. A small-claims court held that 30A's provisions are "בעלות אופי צרכני מובהק... הוראות קוגנטיות". It said an advertiser must meet the conditions "ככתבן וכלשונן, ואין לאפשר עקיפה של ההוראות בדרכים יצירתיות". It cited the Tel Aviv District Court in ת"א 1586/09 איילת חיות נ' טלרן (5.4.2011). — [ת"ק (ת"א) 22190-09-12 מזן נ' לוג טק תקשורת, Tel Aviv Small Claims, 13.3.2013 (non-binding)](https://www.consumers.org.il/files/2013/court/22190-09-12.doc)
- From the brief, not re-read by me: Bat Yam Magistrate 49111-10-22 (2.8.2023) held that a message that already contains the advertising is not a one-time consent request. Hadera Small Claims 35387-09-11 (2011) held that a first approach to a business was lawful and a second one without consent was a violation. Both are non-binding. A search for the Hadera full text found nothing. — [Brief, section 3](../../docs/legal-research-questions.md)

### Inferences
- The plan matches every textual element if (a) the recipient really is a business, (b) only one approach is ever made, through one channel, and (c) the text has no advertising content beyond the offer to agree. Element (c) belongs to the separate message-text research (B1). The 2007 warning about "consent requests" that carried the whole ad shows that courts will look at the content.
- Practical point: email to a business address published on the business's own site is the cleanest fit with the statute's wording. Courts read 30A strictly, so every condition must be met literally.

### Gaps
- No ruling that applied the exception to an email that was only a consent request, and found it lawful in full reasoning, was read first-hand. The Hadera ruling is known only from a summary.

## A2: Is a contact-form submission an "הודעה אלקטרונית"? Is there a ruling? If not, does 30A apply?

### Takeaway
GRAY. No ruling on contact forms was found. On its words, the definition probably covers a form submission: it is a coded message sent over the internet to a recipient and stored by computer. Posiblo should therefore assume 30A applies, and comply with it, including counting the form submission as the one-time approach.

### Cited Findings
- Definition: "'הודעה אלקטרונית' – מסר בזק מקודד המועבר ברשת האינטרנט אל נמען או קבוצה של נמענים, וניתן לשמירה ולאחזור בדרך ממוחשבת". — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- The Supreme Court said that whether messages on a social network fall within 30A had been discussed in lower courts but "דומה שהדיון בה טרם מוצה". It decided nothing on it. — [רע"א 3599/18 שפירא נ' עופרי, 30.5.2018](https://he.afiklaw.com/caselaw/15724)
- The Minister may add more channels by order: "(יב) השר, באישור ועדת הכלכלה של הכנסת, רשאי לקבוע דרכים נוספות של שיגור דבר פרסומת באמצעות מיתקן בזק או ציוד קצה שהוראות סעיף זה יחולו לגביהן." — [Nevo, s. 30A(יב)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Searches of law.co.il, isoc.org.il and general web search found no ruling on advertising sent through a site's "צור קשר" form. The isoc.org.il spam-ruling index page shows no entry on contact forms. — [isoc.org.il spam verdicts](https://www.isoc.org.il/freedom-of-internet/spam/spam-verdicts)

### Inferences
- A form submission is sent over the internet (HTTP) to the site owner. It is normally stored in a database or forwarded as email, so it can be "saved and retrieved by computer". Many form plugins actually deliver it to the business as an email. A court is therefore likely to treat it as an "הודעה אלקטרונית", and the strict, consumer-protective reading in the case law points the same way.
- If a court held that a form is not an "הודעה אלקטרונית", the 30A(ב) ban (and so the exception) would not apply to it at all. The Privacy Protection Law's direct-mail rules would still apply (other researcher's scope). Planning on that reading would be unsafe.
- Practical point: treat the form submission as a 30A message. Put "פרסומת" at the start (and in the subject field, if there is one), include the 30A(ה) details, and treat it as the business's single approach.

### Gaps
- No ruling on contact forms exists in the sources searched. Nevo's full ruling database (paywalled) was not searched.

## A3: Who counts as a "בית עסק"?

### Takeaway
UNKNOWN or GRAY. The law has no definition of "בית עסק", and no court test was found. The legislative history contrasts a "business" with a "residence or private sphere". One non-binding ruling held, in passing, that a blogger's mailbox is not a business's mailbox. Rulings on sole proprietors, clinics, lawyers, nonprofits, businesses using personal Gmail, or platform pages: none found.

### Cited Findings
- Section 30A(א) defines "דבר פרסומת", "הודעה אלקטרונית", "מפרסם" and other terms, but not "בית עסק". — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Committee, 27.11.2007: "אני מבקש לציין שמדובר בבית עסק, לא בית מגורים." Reply: "נכון, בית עסק בלבד." — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- The Ministry of Justice representative (Dana Neufeld) explained the distinction: "הפנייה שלך לאדם הביתה היא פנייה שהיא ברורה, שהיא לתוך התחום הפרטי שלו, להבדיל מבית עסק... ככל שמדובר בפנייה אליי הביתה או לטלפון הסלולרי הפרטי שלי, את מטרידה אותי". — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- A small-claims court, in an obiter remark, held that a blog operator's mailbox is not a business's: "תיבת הדוא"ל של התובעת אינה מהווה תיבת דוא"ל של 'בית עסק', שהרי גורם המפעיל בלוג אינו מהווה 'עסק'". — [ת"ק (ת"א) 22190-09-12 מזן נ' לוג טק, 13.3.2013 (non-binding)](https://www.consumers.org.il/files/2013/court/22190-09-12.doc)
- A lawyer MK in committee complained that every employee's mailbox gets "one-time" offers ("יש לי משרד עורכי דין"). This shows the drafters had law offices in mind as business recipients, but it is debate, not a ruling. — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- For comparison, the Consumer Protection Authority's guide to the do-not-call registry says a business can also be a consumer: "עצם העובדה שהצרכן יכול להיות גם עוסק ומספר הטלפון יכול להיות כמספר טלפון של עסק, לא גורעת מהאינטרס הצרכני". It then exempts calls "באמצעות מספר טלפון המפורסם כמספר טלפון של העוסק, על מנת להציע לו שירות עסקי באופן מובהק". This applies the Consumer Protection Law, not 30A. — [Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority, guide for businesses, "אל תתקשרו אליי"](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf)

### Inferences
- The test the sources suggest is functional: is the approach made to the recipient in its business capacity, at a business contact point, rather than into a private sphere? A contact form on the business's own website, or an email address the business publishes there as its business contact, is the strongest case.
- Sole proprietor, freelancer, one-person clinic, lawyer, accountant: probably a "בית עסק" when reached at the business's published business contact. No ruling confirms this. GRAY.
- A business using a personal Gmail (for example a first name plus surname address): higher risk, because the address may be read as a private mailbox (the "private sphere" rationale). GRAY, and riskier than info@ on the business's own domain.
- Nonprofit (עמותה): UNKNOWN. The 2016 words "לנמען לשם קבלת תרומה או תעמולה" describe the purpose of the advertiser's approach, not the recipient's type. Nothing found says whether an עמותה is a "בית עסק". Excluding nonprofits, or treating them as high risk, is the cautious choice.
- Business pages on platforms: a Wix site with its own form or email is the same as any business site. A Facebook page message is a different channel (see F4).
- Practical point: send only to contact points the business itself publishes as business contacts on its own site, prefer generic or role addresses on the business's own domain, and skip personal-looking free-mail addresses unless a lawyer approves them.

### Gaps
- No ruling defining "בית עסק" for 30A, or deciding on sole proprietors, clinics, nonprofits or Gmail addresses, was found. Nevo's ruling search was not available.

## A4/A5: Does "חד-פעמית" mean one approach across all channels, or one per channel? If form delivery is uncertain, may an email follow?

### Takeaway
GRAY, but the text points to one approach in total. The exception covers a single approach made "באחת הדרכים" (in one of the ways), not one approach per way. A follow-up email after a form whose delivery is uncertain risks being a second approach. Treat any attempted form submission as the approach, as the tool already does with `sent_unconfirmed`.

### Cited Findings
- Statutory wording: "פנייה חד-פעמית ... באחת הדרכים האמורות בסעיף קטן זה". — [Nevo, s. 30A(ב)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- The pre-2008 fax-only version ("פניה חד-פעמית ... באמצעות פקסימילה") had only one channel. When amendment 40 added the other channels, the wording became "באחת הדרכים". — [Nevo, s. 30A version history](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Committee debate: "כל אחד שולח פעם, כל בית עסק שולח פעם" and "כל אחד שישלח רק פעם אחת". — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- From the brief, summary only: Hadera Small Claims 35387-09-11 held that a second approach without consent was a violation. Non-binding. — [Brief, section 3](../../docs/legal-research-questions.md)

### Inferences
- "One-time approach, by one of the channels" is most naturally read as one approach in total, through a single channel. A reading of one per channel would allow four approaches (fax, dialer, email, SMS), which goes against the purpose stated in committee.
- If a form POST was sent but delivery is unconfirmed, the business may well have received it. A later email could then be a second approach, outside the exception and exposed to up to ₪1,000 per message (30A(י)). The safer rule: any submitted POST uses up that business's single approach. An email should follow only when it is certain nothing was delivered, for example when the request never left the client or was refused before submission. Even then, a lawyer should confirm (G9).
- Practical point: keep the ledger's "one approach per business across all channels" rule. Mark `sent_unconfirmed` as final. Do not add an email fallback for `sent_unconfirmed`.

### Gaps
- No ruling on a form plus email combination, or on failed-delivery retries, was found.

## A6: A business with several websites, emails or branches: one recipient or several?

### Takeaway
GRAY. The law does not say. In committee, the legal adviser raised a proposal to state that the one-time approach goes "to one address only" of the business. The final text does not contain those words, and the transcript does not show it was adopted. Treat the business (the legal entity behind the sites) as one recipient and approach one address only.

### Cited Findings
- An Internet Association participant (חיים רביה) said: "צריך שיהיה ברור גם מנוסח החוק, שפנייה חד-פעמית היא לכתובת אחת ויחידה של אותו בית עסק, ולא שאלפי עובדיו של בית העסק יתחילו להיות מופצצים בפניות חד-פעמיות." — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- Committee legal adviser Etti Bendler: "נמען שהוא בית עסק – היתה כאן הצעה שיהיה ברור שניתן יהיה לפנות לבית העסק פעם אחת בלבד, לכתובת אחת בלבד... האם רוצים להבהיר את הנוסח, שכשפונים לבית עסק לא יפנו למספר כתובת אפשריות של בית העסק." MK Gilad Erdan gave the example of 3,000 employees with 3,000 mailboxes, and MK Binyamin Elon said "חשוב שתהיה איזושהי כתובת, ומי האישיות המשפטית". — [Knesset transcript 27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- The enacted text addresses the "נמען שהוא בית עסק" and says nothing about addresses. — [Nevo, s. 30A(ב)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)

### Inferences
- "נמען שהוא בית עסק" refers to the business itself, not each mailbox or site. Several sites, emails or branches of one legal entity are most safely treated as one recipient. Separate legal entities, such as independent franchisees, are arguably separate recipients. No source confirms this.
- Practical point: dedupe by business identity (name, company or עוסק number where visible, shared contact details) as well as by domain. Approach one address per business.

### Gaps
- No ruling on multi-site or multi-branch recipients was found.

## A7: Does automated sending by software change anything vs. a person typing it?

### Takeaway
For email and contact forms, no: the law turns on the channel and the content, not on automation. OK. Automation matters only for voice calls (the "מערכת חיוג אוטומטי" rule) and, in practice, for scale, which affects damages and the risk of class actions.

### Cited Findings
- 30A(ב) bans advertising by "פקסימיליה, מערכת חיוג אוטומטי, הודעה אלקטרונית או הודעת מסר קצר". Only the voice channel is defined by automation: "'מערכת חיוג אוטומטי' – מיתקן בזק או ציוד קצה המשמש לחיוג או לניתוב אוטומטיים של רצף שיחות לקבוצה של נמענים לשם העברת מסר קולי מוקלט". — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)-(ב)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- When setting damages, the court must weigh "(ג) היקף ההפרה". There is no defense where the message was sent to an address list "שהורכבו בהתאם לרצף אקראי של אותיות, ספרות או סימנים אחרים". — [Nevo, s. 30A(י)(3), (י)(5)(ג)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)

### Inferences
- An email or form message typed by a person is still an "הודעה אלקטרונית". Software sending does not change whether the exception applies.
- Posiblo takes addresses from each business's own site, not from random generation, so the 30A(י)(5)(ג) no-defense rule does not apply.
- Scale is the real effect. Any error repeats across every recipient, and a court weighs "היקף ההפרה". Pilot batches and volume caps reduce this risk.

### Gaps
- No ruling contrasting automated with manual email under 30A was found. Probably none is needed, given the wording.

## A8: Israeli businesses with .com domains or websites hosted abroad: same law?

### Takeaway
UNKNOWN in terms of a direct source, but very likely the same law applies. Nothing in 30A depends on the domain or hosting location. It applies to an "מפרסם" sending to a "נמען" over the internet, and here both are Israeli.

### Cited Findings
- The definition of "הודעה אלקטרונית" refers to "רשת האינטרנט" with no geographic limit. — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- In committee (31.1.2007), a participant noted that "העבריינים האמיתיים נמצאים בחוץ לארץ". This shows awareness of foreign senders, not foreign hosting. — [Knesset transcript 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)

### Inferences
- The legally relevant facts are an Israeli sender and an Israeli business recipient. A .com domain or foreign hosting does not plausibly take the message outside 30A. Plan as if 30A applies in full.

### Gaps
- No source or ruling on domain or hosting location under 30A was found.

## F1: SMS to a business's published mobile number

### Takeaway
Same exception, legally: SMS ("הודעת מסר קצר") is one of the listed channels. The one-time business exception applies to it in the same way, with simpler required details (30A(ה)(2)). An SMS would use up the business's single approach. Mobile numbers of sole proprietors are often private phones, which raises the "בית עסק" risk (A3).

### Cited Findings
- "'הודעת מסר קצר' – מסר בזק הכולל כתב, לרבות אותות או סימנים, או מסר בזק הכולל חוזי או שמע, ומועבר באמצעות רשת בזק אל ציוד קצה של נמען או קבוצה של נמענים". Amendment 76 (2022) changed "רשת בזק ציבורית" (the 2016 wording) to "רשת בזק". — [Nevo, s. 30A(א) and version history](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- "(2) על אף הוראות פסקה (1), מפרסם המשגר דבר פרסומת באמצעות הודעת מסר קצר יציין בדבר הפרסומת רק את שמו ואת דרכי יצירת הקשר עמו לצורך מתן הודעת סירוב." — [Nevo, s. 30A(ה)(2)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Ministry of Justice in committee: the rule applies to "דף מסר קצר, שזה SMS ודומיו בסלולר"; and "פנייה אליי הביתה או לטלפון הסלולרי הפרטי שלי" was the example of a private-sphere approach. — [Knesset transcripts 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc), [27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)

### Inferences
- Under 30A, SMS is not safer than email. It is riskier where the number is a personal mobile. It counts toward the single approach.

### Gaps
- No ruling on a one-time consent-request SMS to a business was found.

## F2: WhatsApp messages

### Takeaway
GRAY. No ruling was verified. A WhatsApp message travels over the internet and is stored, so it most likely fits "הודעה אלקטרונית". It may also fit the amended SMS definition, which since 2022 covers any "רשת בזק". Either way 30A, and the same one-time exception, probably apply. The Consumer Protection Authority treats WhatsApp chats as "שיחה" for the do-not-call rules.

### Cited Findings
- Definitions of "הודעה אלקטרונית" and "הודעת מסר קצר" as quoted above. — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- The Consumer Protection Authority, applying the Consumer Protection Law's definition "'שיחה' – לרבות בתקשורת אלקטרונית", says: "שיחה היא לא דווקא שיחה קולית, אלא כל תקשורת בין שני צדדים שבה כל צד מצפה לתגובה מיידית של הצד השני (גם התכתבות בוואטסאפ וכדו')". — [CPFTA guide for businesses, do-not-call registry](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf)
- A marketing blog claims that a ruling (about an עמותה) treated WhatsApp messages as advertising sent in breach of 30A. The ruling was not identified or verified. — [powerads.ai blog (unverified, not a primary source)](https://powerads.ai/blog/whatsapp-broadcast-spam-law)

### Inferences
- Plan as if WhatsApp is covered by 30A. If it is, the exception applies the same way and a WhatsApp message uses up the single approach. WhatsApp to a business's published number may also fall under the do-not-call regime (F3), unless it is a clearly business-to-business offer.

### Gaps
- No verified ruling on WhatsApp under 30A.

## F3: Human phone calls, and the "אל תתקשרו אליי" do-not-call registry

### Takeaway
Under 30A: a call made by a person is outside 30A, which covers only automatic dialing. OK, per a district-court decision (non-binding) and the Ministry of Justice in committee. Do-not-call registry: it rests on Consumer Protection Law s. 16ג (amendment 61) and protects "צרכן". The Consumer Protection Authority says a call to a published business number offering a clearly business service is not a "פנייה שיווקית", so it is allowed. That is OK under the regulator's guidance, which is not binding law. The registry has been reported as not operating.

### Cited Findings
- Jerusalem District Court, רת"ק 31757-03-17 אופיר כהן נ' מכבי שירותי בריאות (6.6.2017), refused leave to appeal and held: "שיחה טלפונית אנושית שנעשתה על-ידי גורם משווק אינה נופלת בגדר האיסור שנקבע בחוק הספאם". Known only from a law-firm summary; the full text was not read. Non-binding outside that case. — [Tzemach Schneider & Co. article (secondary)](https://zes.co.il/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%97%D7%94-%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%99%D7%90-%D7%93%D7%91%D7%A8-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%AA/)
- Ministry of Justice in committee: "הטלמרקטינג האנושי נתפס כמשהו שפחות הפך למכת מדינה, ולכן הוא עדיין אפשרי. כשיש לך טלמרקטינג אנושי, אתה יכול לומר לאדם שמולך שאתה לא רוצה לקבל יותר פניות". — [Knesset transcript 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc)
- The CPFTA guide: amendment 61 to the Consumer Protection Law created the registry. "סעיף 16ג לחוק קובע כי עוסק לא יוכל לפנות לצרכן בפנייה שיווקית למספר טלפון שרשום במאגר". "פנייה שיווקית" means "פנייה של עוסק לצרכן באמצעות שיחה למספר הטלפון שלו במטרה להתקשר בעסקה, בין תוך כדי הפנייה ובין במועד מאוחר יותר". — [CPFTA guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf)
- On businesses, the same guide says: "אם לדוגמא, עוסק פונה לעוסק אחר, באמצעות מספר טלפון המפורסם כמספר טלפון של העוסק, על מנת להציע לו שירות עסקי באופן מובהק, אזי פנייה זו אינה פנייה שיווקית בהתאם לסעיף 16ב ולפיכך מותרת." It also says it is forbidden "לפנות לצרכן ולבקש ממנו שיאשר לנציג מכירות מטעמו להתקשר אליו בפניות שיווקיות". — [CPFTA guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf)
- Registry status: Ynet reported the registry "נסגר זמנית". Search results also claimed a State Comptroller follow-up report (June 2026) found it still inactive as of May 2026. That report was not located or read. — [Ynet, registry temporarily closed](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/hym8zq41je)

### Inferences
- A person calling a business's published number is not covered by 30A. It is also not a "פנייה שיווקית" under the do-not-call rules if it offers a clearly business service to a business number. The Privacy Protection Law's direct-mail rules may still apply (other researcher's scope).
- A human call does not use up the 30A single approach, since 30A does not cover it. Whether a later email after a call is a "second approach" under 30A is untested. Courts could still look at the overall pattern.
- Note that the CPFTA guide forbids asking a consumer for consent to marketing calls. That rule is for consumers on the registry. It is a reason to keep phone outreach clearly B2B and limited to published business numbers.

### Gaps
- The full text of רת"ק 31757-03-17 was not read.
- The registry's current operating status as of November 2026 was not confirmed from an official source.

## F4: LinkedIn and other social-media messages to a business page

### Takeaway
GRAY. The Supreme Court said in 2018 that whether social-network messages fall under 30A has "not yet been exhausted". No later ruling was found. Assume they are "הודעה אלקטרונית" and count toward the single approach.

### Cited Findings
- "דומה שהדיון בה טרם מוצה", on whether messages on a social network fall within 30A. The Court also said that on a social network "עצם יצירתם של קשרי החברות כרוכים בהסכמה, ולו מסוימת, לקבלת הודעות כלשהן". That was an observation, not a holding. — [רע"א 3599/18 שפירא נ' עופרי, Supreme Court, 30.5.2018](https://he.afiklaw.com/caselaw/15724)
- The definition of "הודעה אלקטרונית" covers any coded message sent over the internet and stored by computer. — [Nevo, s. 30A(א)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)

### Inferences
- A LinkedIn InMail or Facebook page message is plausibly an "הודעה אלקטרונית". If it is, the one-time business exception applies the same way, and the message uses up the single approach. Platform terms are a separate issue, outside this scope.

### Gaps
- The lower-court rulings on social-network messages referred to in רע"א 3599/18 were not identified or read.

## Practical implications for Posiblo's outreach (derived from the findings above)

### Takeaway
Use exactly one approach per business, across all channels, to one published business contact point. Make it a pure consent request that carries the 30A(ה) details. Email to a role address on the business's own domain fits the exception best. Treat a form submission as an electronic message. Never send a fallback email after a form POST.

### Cited Findings
- Statutory basis: 30A(ב) exception wording, the 30A(ה) required details, and 30A(י) damages of up to ₪1,000 per message. — [Nevo, s. 30A](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/032_002.htm)
- Legislative intent: one first approach, consent only, businesses only, not the private sphere. — [Knesset transcripts 31.1.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_133809.doc), [27.11.2007](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_ptv_134779.doc)
- Courts apply 30A "ככתבן וכלשונן". — [ת"ק (ת"א) 22190-09-12](https://www.consumers.org.il/files/2013/court/22190-09-12.doc)

### Inferences
- Ledger rule: one approach per business identity, not per domain and not per channel. Any attempted POST or email send is final (`sent`/`sent_unconfirmed`). Fits A4–A6.
- Channel order: (1) email to a role or generic address on the business's own domain, or (2) the site's contact form, but never both. Both are "הודעה אלקטרונית" (A2). SMS, WhatsApp and social messages bring no legal gain and carry more risk that the recipient is "private" (F1, F2, F4).
- Recipient filter: business sites only. Skip personal-looking free-mail addresses, nonprofits, and anything that looks like a private person or blog (A3), unless a lawyer clears them.
- Human phone calls to published business numbers, offering a clearly business service, are outside 30A (F3). They are a possible way to reach CAPTCHA-blocked sites. Direct-mail rules under the Privacy Protection Law still need checking (separate scope).
- Questions for the lawyer (G9 candidates):
  1. Is a contact-form submission an "הודעה אלקטרונית" for 30A?
  2. Is a sole proprietor reached at a personal Gmail a "בית עסק"?
  3. Is an עמותה a "בית עסק"?
  4. If a form POST's delivery is unconfirmed, does a later email count as a second approach?
  5. Are several domains of one legal entity one recipient?

### Gaps
- The gov.il spam FAQ, the 2008 explanatory notes, and the Nevo ruling database could not be read. Any of them could refine the A3–A6 answers.
